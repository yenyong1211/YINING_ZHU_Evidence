## Using references in the portfolio

Use academic sources to support a design rationale, participant records to support the relevant finding, and implementation records to demonstrate what was built. A paper motivating a feature does not prove that the feature improved understanding. A manufacturer statement also does not verify that the student prototype implements that capability.

For uncertain or configuration-dependent product information, record the uncertainty rather than turning missing information into a claim of safety or risk. Do not mix scores or retention claims from different camera models or prototype versions.
