## 3. Stage 2 — After Viewing the Scoring Rules and Evidence

Q11. Re-explain the Rating System

| Concept | Post-guide explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Data Access Exposure | Counts listed pathways involving other people or external services; lower means fewer confirmed exposure pathways. |
| User Control | Measures documented choices such as access, storage, deletion, retention and data use; higher means more documented control. |
| Score range | Occurs when some factors are unknown, so a range is shown. |
| Question mark | Means evidence is insufficient; it does not mean zero exposure or no access. |

**Participant:** “Before, I thought the score was more like your judgement of how private the camera is. Now I understand it is based on specific factors.”

Q12. Final Product Choice

Final choice: eufyCam S3 Pro — unchanged

**Participant:** “I would still choose eufy, but now I am more confident because I can see why it got 5 for exposure and 8 for control.”

Important observation: The participant said Tapo’s 3–7 initially looked positive because 3 is low, but after the guide they understood the upper bound reflected unresolved evidence.

Q13. Higher Exposure vs Stronger Control

**Participant:** “A little higher exposure could be okay if I get much stronger control, but I would want to know why the exposure is higher and whether I can turn things off.”

Boundary: Manufacturer access without permission remained unacceptable even if User Control was otherwise high.
