# Initial Interview: P1

**Source file:** `interviews_1.docx`

---

## Participant Background

Age: 26

Gender: Female

Home situation: Lives with family and has a dog

Number of cameras: 2

Main uses: One camera is used to check on the dog, and another is used to check on an elderly family member

Usage frequency: Checks the dog camera very frequently, around once every hour

## 1. CCTV Usage

**Q:** Have you ever used a home CCTV or smart security camera? Can you tell me a little about how you usually use it?

**A:** Yes. We have two smart cameras at home. One is mainly used to check on our dog, and the other one is used to check on an elderly family member.

**Follow-up:** How often do you check the camera?

**A:** I check the one for my dog quite often. Sometimes I just miss my dog when I'm outside, so I'll open the app and have a look. I would say I probably check it about once every hour.

**Follow-up:** How do you normally watch the CCTV?

**A:** The camera has its own app. We can open the app on our phones and watch the live video directly.

## 2. Data Storage

**Q:** Where do you think your CCTV recordings are stored?

**A:** I think they are normally stored on the memory card inside the camera.

**Follow-up:** Do you know how long the recordings are kept?

**A:** I think it's around seven days, but I'm not completely sure.

**Follow-up:** Do you know whether it uses cloud storage or local storage?

**A:** I'm not completely sure. I know we have a memory card, so I think the recordings are mainly stored there. But because I can watch the camera remotely through the app, I think the internet or cloud might also be involved.

## 3. Data Access

**Q:** Who do you think can watch your CCTV recordings?

**A:** Me and my wife. We both have access to the camera through the app.

**Follow-up:** Do you think anyone else can access the recordings?

**A:** I don't really want anyone else to be able to access them. I'm quite worried about my privacy being leaked.

**Q:** What about the CCTV company or its employees? Do you think they can access your recordings?

**A:** I'm not sure whether they technically can, but I definitely wouldn't want them to access my recordings without my permission. I would be worried about my privacy.

**Follow-up:** If the company accessed your recordings, would you want to know?

**A:** Yes, definitely. I wouldn't want them to secretly access my recordings without telling me.

If I found out that a company had accessed or used my private recordings without my permission, I would be very angry. I would consider taking legal action.

## 4. Privacy Information

**Q:** When you installed or set up the CCTV, did you read any information about privacy?

**A:** No, I didn't really read it.

## 5. Trust

**Q:** What would make you lose trust in a CCTV company?

**A:** If the company stole or accessed my recordings without my permission, especially if they uploaded my private recordings online, I would completely lose trust in them.

**Follow-up:** Would a data leak change how you use the camera?

**A:** Yes, probably.

At the very least, I wouldn't put the camera in highly private places, such as the bedroom. I would be much more careful about where the camera is placed.

**Follow-up:** What would the company need to do to keep your trust?

**A:** I think the company needs to improve its privacy and security protection. It should make sure that other people cannot easily access users' recordings.

## 6. Storage Preferences

**Q:** Would you prefer to store CCTV recordings yourself in the cloud?

**A:** For our needs, we don't really need to store a large amount of CCTV footage.

Usually, if there is a particular moment or clip that we want to keep, we can save that ourselves.

So I would prefer to only keep the recordings that are important to us. I don't think we need to permanently store everything.

Users may value control over retention rather than simply longer storage.

## 7. Transparency Needs

**Q:** Is there anything you would like to know more clearly about where your CCTV videos go or who can see them?

**A:** I think I would feel safer if the manufacturer clearly told us what happens to our recordings.

For example, if they could clearly tell us that recordings will be automatically deleted after a certain period of time, I would feel more secure and reassured.
