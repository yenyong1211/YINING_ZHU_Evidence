## Interview transcript

**Interviewer:** What CCTV brand do you currently use, and how long have you been using it?

**Participant:** I use Hikvision cameras through the Hik-Connect app. I have five cameras installed in my reptile store and have used the system for about three years. They cover the reptile areas and the main store.

**Interviewer:** What functions do you use most often in your CCTV app?

**Participant:** I mainly use live view to check the reptiles and the store when I am away. I also use playback to review customer traffic and what happened during the day.

**Interviewer:** Can you describe how you normally use your CCTV app, from opening it to finishing your task?

**Participant:** I open Hik-Connect, check whether all cameras are online, and select the camera for the area I want to see, such as the reptile area, entrance, or main store. If I need earlier footage, I choose a date and time in playback.

**Interviewer:** What do you like most about your current CCTV app?

**Participant:** Remote access is the main benefit. I can check the store and reptiles from home or elsewhere, and all five cameras are available in one app.

**Interviewer:** What do you dislike or find frustrating about your current CCTV app?

**Participant:** Some settings are quite technical, particularly device sharing and permissions. It is not always easy to understand who can access each camera and what they are allowed to do.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything you wish your current CCTV app could do better?

**Participant:** I would like clearer permission management and better device status information. If a camera goes offline, I want to know when it disconnected and what might have caused it. A clear activity history would also help.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever looked for privacy-related information in your CCTV app?

**Participant:** Yes. Because the cameras record customers, staff, and reptile areas, I have checked account security and device-sharing settings. I want to know who can remotely access cameras and recordings.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever changed any privacy or security settings in your CCTV app?

**Participant:** Yes. I have changed the password, checked connected devices, and reviewed shared access. Some options take time to understand.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever needed to check or manage who can access your cameras or recordings?

**Participant:** Yes. Staff sometimes need access to cameras covering the main store or entrance, but I do not want everyone to access every camera or change device settings. I would prefer to manage permissions in one place.

**Interviewer:** What notifications or activity information does your CCTV app currently provide?

**Participant:** I receive device and event notifications, including when a camera goes offline. I would also like a clear record of account logins, video access, and settings changes.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever noticed anything unusual or unexpected when using your CCTV app?

**Participant:** A camera covering part of the reptile area once went offline while I was away. The app showed it was offline, but I wanted to know why and when it was last connected.

**Interviewer:** What are the three most important things a good CCTV app should provide?

**Participant:** Reliable remote access, clear permission management, and useful activity records. I need to check the store, understand who has access, and see important changes or events.

**Interviewer:** How would you prefer to access CCTV privacy functions?

**Participant:** I would prefer them within my existing CCTV app. Ideally, one privacy or security page would show user permissions, remote access, connected devices, and recent activity. I would consider another tool only if it were secure and trustworthy.
