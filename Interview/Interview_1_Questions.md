Hi, we're doing a university design project about home CCTV systems. We're interested in how people understand CCTV data and privacy. There are no right or wrong answers — we're interested in your own experience and understanding.

## 1.  CCTV Usage

Have you ever used a home CCTV or smart security camera? Can you tell me a little about how you use it?

Follow-up:

What do you mainly use it for?

How often do you check the recordings?

## 2.  Data Storage ⭐

Where do you think your CCTV videos are stored?

Follow-up:

How do you know that?

Do you know if your CCTV uses cloud storage or local storage?

Do you know how long the recordings are kept?

## 3.  Data Access ⭐⭐⭐

Who do you think can watch your CCTV recordings?

Follow-up:

Do you think anyone else can see them besides you?

Why do you think that?

What about the CCTV company? Do you think they can see your recordings?

Follow-up:

In what situation do you think they should be allowed to access it?

If the company accessed your recordings, would you want to know? Why?

## 4.  Privacy Information

When you set up your CCTV, did you read any information about privacy?

Follow-up:

Was it easy to understand?

What do you remember from it?

Why didn't you read it?

## 5.  Trust ⭐

What would make you lose trust in a CCTV company?

Follow-up:

Would a data leak change how you use the camera?

What would the company need to do to keep your trust?

## 6.  Transparency Needs

Is there anything you would like to know about where your CCTV videos go or who can see them?:

Follow-up:

Why is that information important to you?

How would you like this information to be shown to you?

## 7. Mental Model Activity:

Ask the participant to draw their own explanation:

Imagine you are outside your home and you open the CCTV app on your phone to watch your camera. Can you quickly draw how you think the video gets from the camera to your phone? There is no right or wrong answer.
