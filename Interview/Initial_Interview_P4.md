Participant 4 — Parent / Family CCTV User

## Participant Background

Age: 52

Gender: Female

Role: Mother

Child: 19 years old

Living situation: Lives with her husband and child

Started using CCTV: Around 7 years ago, when her child was about 12

Current CCTV experience: Long-term home CCTV user

Main reason for installation: Originally installed to check on her child when both parents were at work, particularly during weekends and school holidays

Current use: Mainly used to remotely check the general situation at home

Access: Mainly herself and her husband

Usage frequency: Sometimes once a day, sometimes once every few days

Technical knowledge: Comfortable with basic CCTV functions but has limited understanding of data storage and access

## 1. CCTV Usage

**Q:** Have you ever used a home CCTV or smart security camera? Can you tell me a little about how you use it?

**A:** 

Yes. We've had a camera at home for quite a long time.

We installed it around seven years ago, when my child was about 12. At that time, both my husband and I had to work, so during weekends and school holidays there were times when our child would be home without us.

We mainly wanted to be able to check what was happening at home when we were at work.

**Follow-up:** What did you mainly use it for at that time?

**A:** 

At the beginning, it was mainly for checking on my child.

When we were both working, especially during school holidays, we couldn't always be at home. So having the camera made it easier for us to check whether everything was okay at home.

Sometimes we would also check whether our child was studying or what they were doing.

**Follow-up:** How do you use it now?

**A:** 

Now my child is already 19, so we don't really need to use the camera to check on them anymore.

But the camera is still at home. Usually I just open the app on my phone and check what's happening at home.

**Follow-up:** How often do you check it now?

**A:** 

It depends. Sometimes I check it once a day, and sometimes I don't check it for a few days.

I mainly check it when I want to see what's happening at home.

## 2. Data Storage

**Q:** Where do you think your CCTV recordings are stored?

**A:** 

I think they're probably stored in the CCTV app.

Because when I want to see the camera, I open the app on my phone, so I always assumed the recordings were stored there.

**Follow-up:** Do you know whether it uses cloud storage or local storage?

**A:** 

No, I don't really understand that.

I know how to open the app and use the camera, but I don't really know whether the videos are stored locally or in the cloud.

**Follow-up:** Do you know how long the recordings are kept?

**A:** 

I'm not completely sure. I think the storage time can be set by the user, but I wasn't the person who set it up, so I don't know exactly how long our recordings are kept.

## 3. Data Access

**Q:** Who do you think can view your CCTV recordings?

**A:** 

Mainly my family. My husband and I can access the camera and view the recordings.

**Follow-up:** Do you think the CCTV manufacturer or its employees can access your recordings?

**A:** 

I don't think they should be able to.

If the manufacturer or its employees could access our recordings whenever they wanted, wouldn't that be a privacy issue?

**Follow-up:** Do you know whether they technically can access the recordings?

**A:** 

I'm actually not sure whether they technically can. I just don't think they should be allowed to access private recordings from inside our home.

**Follow-up:** If the company accessed your recordings, would you want to know?

**A:** 

Yes, definitely.

If I found out that the company had accessed my recordings without my permission, I would want to know about it, and I would complain to them.

## 4. Privacy Information

**Q:** When you installed or set up the CCTV, did you read any information about privacy?

**A:** 

No, I didn't read it myself. My husband read the information when we set up the camera.

I mainly just learned how to use the app afterwards. I didn't really look into the privacy information or the technical details myself.

## 5. Trust

**Q:** What would make you lose trust in a CCTV manufacturer?

**A:** 

If the company accessed or took my recordings without my permission, I would lose trust in them.

The camera is inside our home, so I consider those recordings private.

**Follow-up:** What would the company need to do to maintain your trust?

**A:** 

I think the company should clearly explain its privacy rules.

For example, there should be a clear agreement or information explaining how they protect our privacy.

I would want the company to guarantee that they won't take or use my private recordings without permission.

## 6. Transparency Needs

**Q:** Is there anything you would like to understand more clearly about where your CCTV videos go or who can see them?

**A:** 

Not really at the moment.

I haven't really thought much about these questions before.

| Finding | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| F1. CCTV use can change over time. | Originally installed to monitor a child; now mainly used to check the general situation at home. |
| F2. Long-term use does not guarantee understanding of data storage. | Has used CCTV for around 7 years but doesn't understand cloud vs local storage. |
| F3. Users may confuse the interface with the storage location. | Believes recordings are stored “in the app” because that is where she views them. |
| F4. CCTV knowledge can be divided between household members. | Husband handled setup/privacy information while she uses the CCTV. |
| F5. Users distinguish between what companies technically can do and what they should be allowed to do. | She is unsure about manufacturer access but strongly believes they should not access private recordings without permission. |
| F6. Limited knowledge does not automatically create demand for more information. | Despite uncertainty about storage/access, she said there was currently nothing more she wanted to know. |
