# Initial Interview: P2

**Source file:** `interviews_1.docx`

---

## Participant Background

Age: 21

Gender: Female

Home situation: Lives with family and has a dog

Number of cameras: 3

Main uses: One camera is used to check on the dog, and another is used to check on an elderly family member

Usage frequency: Checks the dog camera very frequently, around once every hour

## 1. CCTV Usage

**Q:** Have you ever used a home CCTV or smart security camera?

**A:** Yes, we use smart cameras at home.

**Q:** Can you briefly explain how you usually use them?

**A:** We mainly use them to check on our dog when nobody is at home. My parents sometimes open the camera when they have time just to see what the dog is doing.

We also use cameras to check on our elderly family members, such as our grandparents. Because they are getting older, sometimes we're worried about their safety when they are home alone. We can check whether they're okay or whether something has happened, such as a fall.

**Follow-up:** How often do you check the camera?

**A:** There's no fixed schedule. Usually, if I have some free time, I'll open the app and have a quick look. We probably check it more often when the dog or an elderly family member is home alone.

## 2. Data Storage

**Q:** Where do you think your CCTV recordings are stored?

**A:** I think they're probably stored in the cloud.

**Follow-up:** Do you know whether your CCTV uses cloud storage or local storage?

**A:** I'm not completely sure. I think it probably uses cloud storage, but I've never really looked into it.

**Follow-up:** Do you know how long the recordings are kept?

**A:** No, I don't actually know. I've never checked how long the recordings are stored.

## 3. Data Access

**Q:** Who do you think can view your CCTV recordings?

**A:** In our family, anyone who has the app installed and has permission to access the camera can view it.

**Follow-up:** Do you think the CCTV manufacturer or its employees can access your recordings?

**A:** I think they might be able to access them in some specific situations.

**Follow-up:** In what situations do you think they should be allowed to access them?

**A:** Maybe if there is a technical or security problem.

For example, if something goes wrong with the system or we lose some data, perhaps the company could help us recover or find the recordings.

But I don't think they should normally access our recordings without a reason.

**Q:** If the company accessed your recordings without your permission, would you want to know?

**A:** Definitely. I think I have the right to know if someone accesses my CCTV recordings.

CCTV recordings contain a lot of private information about our home and family, so I would want to know if the company accessed them, especially if I hadn't given permission.

## 4. Privacy Information

**Q:** When you installed or set up the CCTV, did you read any information about privacy protection?

**A:** Not really. When we set it up, we mainly looked at the instructions about how to install and use the camera.

We didn't really pay much attention to the privacy information.

## 5. Trust

**Q:** What would make you lose trust in a CCTV manufacturer?

**A:** If the company accessed our home CCTV recordings without my permission, I would lose trust in them.

It would be even worse if our private recordings were leaked or uploaded online. That would be a serious privacy problem for me.

**Follow-up:** Would a data leak change the way you use the camera?

**A:** Yes, definitely. If there was a serious data leak, I might even consider stopping using the camera completely.

**Q:** What would the manufacturer need to do to maintain your trust?

Cleaned Answer

**A:** I think users should have the right to know if the company wants to access their CCTV data.

If the company needs to access a customer's recordings, they should let the customer know and get permission first.

They also need to protect users' recordings and privacy properly.

## 6. Transparency Needs

**Q:** Is there anything you would like to understand more clearly about your CCTV privacy?

**A:** I would mainly like to know more clearly who can access the CCTV recordings from my home.

I know that people in my family with permission can access them, but I would like clearer information about whether anyone else, such as the company, can also access them.

**Follow-up:** Why is this important to you?

**A:** Because the camera records things happening inside our home. I think we should know who has access to that information.

## 7. Mental Model Activity

Task

Imagine you're outside your home and open the CCTV app on your phone. Can you draw how you think the video gets from the camera to your phone?

**A:** I'm not sure. This is quite difficult.

I know that I can open the app on my phone and watch the camera, but I don't really know how the video actually gets from the camera to the app.
