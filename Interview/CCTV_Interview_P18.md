## Interview transcript

**Interviewer:** What CCTV brand do you currently use, and how long have you been using it?

**Participant:** I use Xiaomi CCTV cameras at home and have used them for about five years. There are three cameras: two indoors and one outdoors.

**Interviewer:** What functions do you use most often, and how frequently?

**Participant:** I mainly use live view and the two-way talk function, approximately once a week.

**Interviewer:** Can you describe how you normally use your CCTV app, from opening it to finishing your task?

**Participant:** I unlock my phone, open the CCTV app, choose one of the three cameras by name, and view its live footage. Sometimes a network delay prevents the video from connecting.

**Interviewer:** What do you like most about your current CCTV app?

**Participant:** I like the alerts when something unusual happens at home while I am away. They help me understand whether my home is safe.

**Interviewer:** What do you dislike or find frustrating about your current CCTV app?

**Participant:** Sometimes the green indicator for the two-way talk function lights up even when neither I nor my parents are using the app. It happens rarely, but we have not resolved it.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything you wish your current CCTV app could do better?

**Participant:** I would like smarter, more accurate detection of human movement.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever looked for privacy-related information in your CCTV app?

**Participant:** I have opened a privacy notice, but I have not actively looked for much more information.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever changed any privacy or security settings in your CCTV app?

**Participant:** I do not think I have changed these settings.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever needed to check or manage who can access your cameras or recordings?

**Participant:** Yes. Only my parents and I should have access. To add someone, they install the same app, search for the shared camera name, and enter a password.

**Interviewer:** What notifications or activity information does your CCTV app currently provide?

**Participant:** It provides alerts or records for unusual activity, such as someone walking through the living room at night.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever noticed anything unusual or unexpected when using your CCTV app?

**Participant:** Sometimes the camera cannot connect to the network. Occasionally, the green two-way-talk indicator also lights up when nobody is operating the app.

**Interviewer:** What are the three most important things a good CCTV app should provide?

**Participant:** Real-time video, intelligent security alerts, and good privacy protection.

**Interviewer:** How would you prefer to access CCTV privacy functions?

**Participant:** I would like privacy controls to be clearly visible on the app’s main page, so they are easy to find and manage.

**Interviewer:** How should privacy information and informed consent be presented?

**Participant:** I would like a notice when the app is first used and when each camera is first activated. I would also like short explanations with text and images or icons.
