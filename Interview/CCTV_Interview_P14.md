## Interview transcript

**Interviewer:** What CCTV brand do you currently use, and how long have you been using it?

**Participant:** I use a Xiaomi CCTV camera. I have been using it for almost one year.

**Interviewer:** What functions do you use most often in your CCTV app, and why?

**Participant:** I mainly use live view and sometimes record videos. The main reason is to check on my dog when nobody is home.

**Interviewer:** Can you describe how you normally use your CCTV app, from opening it to finishing your task?

**Participant:** I open the app and watch what my dog is doing, such as eating or sleeping. If I see a cute moment, I take a screenshot or record it, then close the app. The live view appears when I open the app.

**Interviewer:** What do you like most about your current CCTV app?

**Participant:** I like being able to see the live video immediately. It is also convenient to take a screenshot using the button in the app instead of my phone’s screenshot function.

**Interviewer:** What do you dislike or find frustrating about your current CCTV app?

**Participant:** Sometimes the app sends a motion alert even though everyone is in a bedroom outside the camera’s view. These unexpected alerts can be frightening.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything you wish your current CCTV app could do better?

**Participant:** I would like more accurate motion alerts and better pet tracking. The camera is supposed to follow my dog, but sometimes it responds slowly and does not turn quickly enough.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever looked for privacy-related information in your CCTV app?

**Participant:** I have seen the settings, but I have not thought much about whether other people can see the footage. When setting up the camera, I understood that recordings were saved locally. The settings seem fairly simple.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever changed any privacy or security settings in your CCTV app?

**Participant:** Not in this CCTV app. I also have another camera in a pet-feeding device, and I may have turned that camera off at certain times.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever needed to check or manage who can access your cameras or recordings?

**Participant:** Two people in our household share access to the CCTV view. We have not given anyone else permission.

**Interviewer:** What notifications or activity information does your CCTV app currently provide?

**Participant:** It sends a notification when movement is detected. I can tap the notification to see the recording from that time.

**Interviewer:** Have you ever noticed anything unusual or unexpected when using your CCTV app?

**Participant:** Apart from the unexpected motion alerts when nobody appears to be in view, I have not noticed other major problems.

**Interviewer:** What are the most important things a good CCTV app should provide?

**Participant:** For our household, responsive pet tracking and clear night-time video are important. I also value strong privacy protection and the ability to connect the camera with other devices, such as viewing the live feed in our car.

**Interviewer:** How would you prefer to access CCTV privacy functions?

**Participant:** I think privacy depends partly on how much the company can be trusted. I would like a simpler interface and strong protection against privacy leaks. I did not specify an exact location for these controls.
