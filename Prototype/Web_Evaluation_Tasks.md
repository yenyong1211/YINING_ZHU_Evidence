# Proposed Evaluation Tasks — Connected Web Prototype

**Status: proposed evaluation, not completed findings.**

Adapted from Section 5.2 of the A1.3 final report. The completed label studies did not evaluate the full connected AIRIS–eufy journey.

## Participants and procedure

Recruit household CCTV owners who manage shared access, with different levels of familiarity with privacy settings. Include shared members when evaluating their permissions and activity visibility. Use fictional accounts and camera scenes. Ask participants to explain their reasoning while completing goal-based tasks. Record an independent attempt before offering help, and distinguish completion after a hint from independent completion.

## Tasks

| Task | Evidence to collect | Essential understanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find where footage storage is explained and locate a relevant setting | Navigation route, wrong destinations, independent completion and assistance | Guidance and the demonstrated control have different roles |
| Explain local storage and remote viewing | Participant explanation and configuration assumptions | Storage location and transmission are different questions |
| Identify a shared member's effective access | Selected member, camera, visibility state and management restrictions | Permissions apply to a particular person and camera |
| Change a member's permission and explain its consequences | Completion, resulting state and explanation | Future access changes do not erase earlier visits or downloaded copies |
| Interpret activity events | Explanation of each displayed event and uncertainty | Recording history, viewing records and failed verification are different events |

Only use event scenarios supported by the tested build. A failed-verification scenario may need a separate mock-up: the supplied local Access History view reads seeded visits rather than a verified combined event stream.

## Measures

Record completion, explanation accuracy, critical errors, assistance, time and confidence. Faster completion is not sufficient evidence of understanding. Compare confidence with accuracy to identify confident misunderstandings.

The report proposes an initial formative study of eight participants, with provisional targets of at least six independent completions and six correct explanations per task. These are proposed targets, not achieved outcomes or population estimates.

## Revision triggers

Revise and retest if users believe that revoking access deletes downloaded footage, that masking destroys original recordings, or that a failed verification proves successful camera access. Also compare the third-party guidance route with an equivalent in-app explanation to investigate whether the additional transition helps or adds effort.
